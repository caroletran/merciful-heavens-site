Our Mission

We believe a holiday table is a place of dignity — not a luxury.

Every November, our neighbors, restaurant family, and local volunteers come together to source fresh produce and pantry staples in bulk, hand-pack them into grocery bags, and deliver them directly to families throughout Jupiter who could use the extra support. No paperwork, no lines — just a full bag and a "thank you," family to family.

How It Works

From the loading dock to the front porch.

Every bag passes through the same hands-on process, run almost entirely by volunteers and local donors.

01

We Source

Truckloads of fresh produce, potatoes, and pantry staples are sourced in bulk from local growers and suppliers.

02

We Sort

Volunteers unload and sort everything by hand — vegetables, canned goods, and holiday extras — ready for packing.

03

We Pack

Every bag is filled with a full Thanksgiving spread, from cranberry sauce to fresh corn, and labeled with care.

04

We Deliver

Bags go out by the truckload to families across Jupiter — family to family, porch to porch.
