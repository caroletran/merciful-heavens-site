Our Mission

We believe a holiday table is a place of dignity — not a luxury.

We work with local agencies throughout Jupiter and Palm Beach County to identify families in need. Every November, our neighbors, restaurant family, and local volunteers come together to source fresh produce and pantry staples in bulk and hand-pack them into grocery bags, turkey included. Families pick up their meals in person, partner agencies collect meals for the families they serve, and we deliver to those who can't make it. Just a full Thanksgiving meal, handed over with dignity, family to family.

Thanksgiving is our biggest drive of the year, but our mission is fighting food insecurity in our community year-round.

How It Works

From the loading dock to the front porch.

Every bag passes through the same hands-on process, run almost entirely by volunteers and local donors.

01

We Source

Truckloads of fresh produce, potatoes, and pantry staples are sourced in bulk from local growers and suppliers.

02

We Sort

Volunteers unload and sort everything by hand — vegetables, canned goods, and holiday extras — ready for packing.

03

We Pack

Every bag is filled with a full Thanksgiving spread, from cranberry sauce to fresh corn.

04

We Distribute

Families pick up their meals in person on distribution day, or a partner agency collects them for the families it serves. We deliver to those who can't make it.
